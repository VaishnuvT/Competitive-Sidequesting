Hackathon Theme: Build it Forward 
This year’s theme challenges you to pick a real campus problem, build a meaningful solution, and create something that could genuinely outlast your time at UT. This is an opportunity to develop tangible skills, strengthen your portfolio, and gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge AI technology while solving problems that matter.
Your app will be judged based on the following categories:
Impact (25%): What is the project’s long-term potential for success, growth and impact? Does it fit into the problem statement? Is the project useful, and for who?
Codex App (25%): Tell a creative, end-to-end story of how the Codex app enabled the build with parallel agents and worktrees
Creative Use of Skills (25%): Strong practical use of skills in Codex for coding workflows, developer tasks, and “non-technical technical work”
Demo & Pitch (25%): Is the concept of this project novel and interesting to watch? Is it presented and demo’d well (NOT just a presentation, figma file, or anything else)? How well has the team implemented the idea?
