# Demo Checklist (Under 1 Minute)

1. Open `/` and tap **Start Sidequesting**.
2. On `/onboarding`, choose this camera-friendly set:
- Interests: `food`, `outdoors`, `study spots`
- Time: `30 min`
- Budget: `under $10`
- Mode: `either`
- Transport: `walk`
- Vibe: `chill`, `spontaneous`
3. Tap **See my sidequests** and wait for 3 cards.
4. Open one quest, show details and the map link.
5. Tap **Complete this quest**, add a short note, and save.
6. Show `/journal` success message and the new entry at the top.
7. If needed, tap **Load demo entries** on `/journal` to show a richer feed.
